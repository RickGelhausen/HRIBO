Coverage track for one sequencing library, as a bigWig file for genome browsers.

The mapping style determines what each read contributes: its full length
(global), its centre with the ends clipped (centered), or only its 5' or 3'
terminal base. Three normalisations are provided: raw counts, counts per million
mapped reads (mil), and counts scaled to the smallest library (min). Forward and
reverse strands are separate files.
